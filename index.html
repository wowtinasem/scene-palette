Instrumental cinematic chamber music for a Regency-era romantic drama. Intimate pianoforte, warm strings and gentle clarinet. Restrained longing, elegant 6/8 pulse, gradual emotional rise, no modern percussion. Begin with quiet distance, bloom during the rain confession, end with a tender resolved theme. Duration 2:30.
